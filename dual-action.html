Mechanism of action

A complementary dual-action approach

RETINOID

Adapalene

Targets the underlying skin processes that drive acne formation.

Adapalene selectively activates RAR-β and RAR-γ to normalize follicular keratinization, reduce microcomedones, and provide anti-inflammatory effects.

ANTIMICROBIAL

Benzoyl Peroxide

Delivers antimicrobial action while promoting gentle exfoliation of the skin surface.

Together, adapalene + benzoyl peroxide address multiple acne pathways simultaneously, delivering superior efficacy over monotherapy.
