American Academy of Dermatology · 2024

AAD treatment principles

Fixed-dose combination

Combines topical therapies with multiple mechanisms of action.

First-line topical agents

Benzoyl peroxide and topical retinoids are strongly recommended first-line topical agents.

Limit systemic antibiotics

Systemic antibiotic use should be limited and combined with benzoyl peroxide/topical therapy when used.

AAD good clinical practice

Combine mechanisms, limit antibiotics

Topical

Combine topical therapies with multiple mechanisms of action.

Combine benzoyl peroxide and other topical therapies with systemic antibiotics.

Systemic

Reserve oral isotretinoin for severe acne that is failing standard oral/topical therapy or is causing significant psychosocial burden or scarring.

Limit systemic antibiotic use.

Combine systemic antibiotics with benzoyl peroxide and other topical therapies.

NICE guidelines: key treatment principles

01

Individualized treatment

Based on acne severity and patient preference, considering the advantages and disadvantages of treatment options.

02Adapalene + BPO

Fixed combination therapy

Adapalene + benzoyl peroxide is a first-line option for any acne severity.

03

Antibiotic stewardship

Avoid topical or oral antibiotic monotherapy and topical + oral antibiotic combinations.

04

12-week review

Review treatment response at 12 weeks and adjust as appropriate.

05

Oral isotretinoin

Consider for severe acne resistant to adequate standard therapy with systemic antibiotics and topical therapy.

06Adapalene + BPO

Maintenance after improvement

Consider a fixed-dose combination of topical adapalene + benzoyl peroxide as maintenance, particularly for patients with a history of frequent relapse.
