Lesion reduction: range across 8 RCTs

Outcome: Across 8 RCTs (n = 4,596), fixed-dose adapalene + benzoyl peroxide demonstrated efficacy, with reported primary efficacy outcomes ranging from 27.5% to 70.2% (p < 0.001).

Range reflects differences in trial design, comparator and duration across the 8 RCTs, not a trend by trial or year.

Clinical study 1 · Study design

Systematic review of 8 randomized controlled trials

Systematic review of 8 randomized controlled trials (2007–2022), 4,596 participants (52.8% female), evaluating adapalene 0.1% or 0.3% combined with benzoyl peroxide 2.5% fixed-dose gel versus monotherapy/vehicle comparators. Trials assessed percent reduction in lesion counts and adverse event rates as primary outcomes; most were multicenter, double-blind, randomized, parallel-group, 12-week studies (some with longer follow-up).
