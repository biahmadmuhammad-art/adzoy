Clinical evidence · Early efficacy

Significantly greater efficacy as early as Week 1 versus monotherapy

A multicenter, randomized, double-blind, controlled trial in 1,668 patients with moderate facial acne.

By Week 12, adapalene 0.1%/BPO 2.5% applied once daily in the evening achieved a significantly higher success rate versus monotherapies (p ≤ 0.006), with a tolerability and safety profile comparable to the monotherapies.

Clinical evidence · 12 months

Sustained efficacy for long-term acne management

In a 12-month multicenter study of 452 patients, once-daily adapalene 0.1%/BPO 2.5% showed long-term safety and efficacy. Skin irritation was mostly mild to moderate, occurred early and decreased over time.

Clinical evidence · Systematic review

Up to 70.2% lesion reduction across 8 RCTs

Across 8 RCTs (n = 4,596), fixed-dose adapalene + benzoyl peroxide demonstrated efficacy, with primary efficacy outcomes ranging from 27.5% to 70.2% (p < 0.001). Adverse effects were generally mild and resolved over time.
