Clinical evidence · Systematic review

Up to 70.2% lesion reduction across 8 RCTs

Across 8 RCTs (n = 4,596) including adapalene 0.1% or 0.3% + BPO 2.5%, fixed-dose adapalene + benzoyl peroxide demonstrated efficacy, with primary efficacy outcomes ranging from 27.5% to 70.2% (p < 0.001).
